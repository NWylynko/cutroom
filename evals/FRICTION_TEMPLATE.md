# Friction report (fill this in as the last thing you do)

Be blunt and specific. The tool's authors will read this to make the tool better for agents like you.
Quote exact commands and output where it helps.

- **Outcome:** done / partly done / failed — one sentence why.
- **Commands run:** count, and the list in order.
- **Errors hit:** each error message, and whether its `fix:` line got you unstuck.
- **Guessed wrong:** flags, fields, commands or concepts you assumed existed or worked differently.
- **Confusing output:** anything you had to re-read, parse awkwardly, or that was too long or too short.
- **Missing:** a command, flag or piece of information you wanted and didn't have.
- **Not sure it's right:** anything about your result you could not verify with the tool.
- **Best part:** what made the task easy.
- **One change:** the single change that would have helped you most.
